# User guide

Aatman has five kinds of users. Everyone can use the **🌐 language selector** at the top of each page to read Aatman in Hindi, Gujarati, Marathi, Tamil or any other language Google Translate supports. Everyone can also ask the **💬 Chat with Aatman** assistant (bottom-right) how to do something, in any language.

## Getting started

1. Click **Sign Up** and enter your name, email, password (at least 8 characters) and phone.
2. Choose who you are: **NGO**, **Donor**, **Person in Need** or **Volunteer**.
3. Fill in the short details form for your role. You are then logged in and taken to your dashboard.
4. Later, use **Login** with your email and password. After 5 wrong passwords, login is paused for 15 minutes.

Every dashboard has a **Profile** tab. Click **Edit**, change your details, then **Save Changes** (or **Cancel**). You can deactivate your account from the bottom of the profile. To reactivate it, contact an administrator.

## 🙏 Person in need

| Tab | What you can do |
|---|---|
| Make a Request | Describe what you need (food, shelter, clothes, medicine, education, other), the quantity, your location, the priority and a contact number |
| My Requests | See your requests. Click **Mark as Completed** once you've been helped, or **Delete** a request |
| View Campaigns | See active NGO campaigns and their contact details |

## 🏢 NGO

| Tab | What you can do |
|---|---|
| View Needy Requests | All pending requests from people in need, highest priority first |
| Create Campaign | Start a drive with a title, category, dates and contact person |
| My Campaigns | Change each campaign's status: **Active**, **Completed** or **Disabled** |
| View Volunteers | Available volunteers, their skills, availability and contact details |
| Request Donors | Post a request that donors and volunteers will see |
| View My Requests | Track, complete or delete your NGO's requests |

## 💝 Donor

| Tab | What you can do |
|---|---|
| Requests | Open requests from people in need and NGOs. Contact them directly to give |
| Campaigns | Active NGO campaigns you can support |
| Profile | Set your donation type (money, goods, services) and preferred cause |

## 🤝 Volunteer

| Tab | What you can do |
|---|---|
| Profile | Set your skills, availability and preferred cause. Switch **Volunteer Status** to "Not available" to hide yourself from NGOs for a while |
| NGO Campaigns | Active campaigns you can join, with the organiser's contact details |
| Open Requests | Requests you could help with |

## 🛡️ Administrator

Admin accounts are created by the site owner (`php scripts/create_admin.php`), never through sign-up.

| Tab | What you can do |
|---|---|
| Overview | Counts of users by role, pending requests and active campaigns |
| Donors / NGOs / Volunteers / People in Need | Full lists. **Deactivate** an account to block login immediately, or **Activate** it again |
| Pending Requests | Every open request on the platform |
| Campaigns | Every campaign. Change any campaign's status |

## Privacy tips

- Aatman staff and the assistant will **never** ask for your password, OTP or bank details.
- Contact details you add to a request are visible to logged-in NGOs, donors and volunteers, so share only what you're comfortable with.
